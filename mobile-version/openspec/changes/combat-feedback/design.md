## Decisions

1. **Real outcomes only.** `CombatRules.resolve_attack()` produces exactly four outcomes: critical (roll at or above the attack's critical threshold, always hits), hit, dodged (the defender's dodge roll succeeded) and miss (the roll did not beat `10 + defender armor - attacker strength`). Since armor is the only thing that makes an attack "miss", the player sees it as **Blocked** ("Kobold's armor stopped your Strike"). There is no separate plain miss, so no "Miss" label is shown. The mapping lives in one place, `CombatFeedback._outcome_from_flags()`.
2. **Damage comes from the rules.** `attack_monster()` and `attempt_flee()` report `player_attack_damage` and `counterattack_damage`, the exact amount `_damage_actor()` subtracted. This is an extra field in the result, not a rule change. The floats and the log use it, so they always match the life bars. The killing blow shows the full damage of the attack, even if the monster had less life left.
3. **Three small units instead of more code in `main.gd`.** `combat_feedback.gd` is static and turns a result into entries `{outcome, target, damage, text}` (translatable, testable without a scene). `fight_log.gd` renders entries. `damage_numbers.gd` floats them. `main.gd` only connects them (`_show_combat_entries()`).
4. **Log order: oldest at the top, newest at the bottom.** A round then reads top-down in the order it happened (your attack, then the monster's reply), and the newest line sits next to the Fight button the player just pressed. Older lines are dimmed to 60 % so the newest one stands out.
5. **Log size: 4 entries** (two rounds of attack and reply). Room for 4 one-line entries is reserved (76 px) so the buttons do not jump as the log fills. A long Spanish line wraps to a second line instead of being cut. With four of the longest lines (critical hits by "Araña gigante" with "Mordida venenosa") the log uses 132 px, ending 10 px above the Fight button on 393x844 and 410x844 logical screens (1440x3088 and 1080x2220 phones). The fighters' area above it expands and shrinks.
6. **Floats layer above the damage overlay, below modals.** The damage overlay dims the whole screen for 0.8 s when the player is hurt. The floats live in a full-screen, input-ignoring layer added right after the overlay, so "-2" stays readable on top of it. Every modal (victory result, monster info, confirmations, stat help, tutorial) is added later and covers it. The layer is cleared when the encounter view hides (victory, escape, depletion), so numbers never float over the map.
7. **Nothing waits for the animation.** Each float is a Tween owned by its label (0.8 s, rises 56 px, fades out), which frees the label at the end. `_fight()` and `_flee_encounter()` return immediately, so the playtest bot and `verify_balance` (which call them directly) are unaffected. A cap of 6 live labels keeps a caller that never lets frames run from piling them up.
8. **Damage overlay text.** The overlay shown when the player is hurt now says what hurt them ("Bat hit you with Bite for 2"). It falls back to the rules message when there is no such line.
9. **Named placeholders.** Log sentences use `{monster}`, `{attack}`, `{damage}` with `String.format()`, so Spanish can order them freely. Spanish lines avoid articles before attack names ("el ataque de Rata (Mordida)"), so they read correctly whatever the attack's gender.
10. **Hint line.** `EncounterFeedback` now only holds the hint for an unaffordable attack and is hidden when empty, so it takes no room in a normal fight.
